PopNDodge - Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 2026

PopNDodge ("the Game") respects user privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how your information is handled when using our application.

1. Information Collection and Use

PopNDodge does NOT collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information (PII), device identifiers, precise location data, or usage analytics. Your privacy is fully respected.

2. Local Data Storage

The Game only stores essential gameplay data (such as high scores, unlocked characters, current level progress, and game settings) locally on your device's internal storage. This data remains on your device and is never transmitted to external servers.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising

PopNDodge uses Google AdMob to serve advertisements (such as Rewarded Ads for extra lives or temporary character unlocks). Google AdMob may collect and use pseudonymous device identifiers, IP addresses, and standard system data to serve ads. You can review Google's Privacy Policy at: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about this Privacy Policy, feel free to contact us at: popndodge@gmail.com
